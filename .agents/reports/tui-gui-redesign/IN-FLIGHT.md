## Prior: b3603cd — real-PTY harness resize-boundary fix
- Investigated the resize-compaction residual left by 5506759 below. Traced `@opentui/core`'s `processResize` (non-split-footer path, what this workbench uses, chunk-node-54dhb2fr.js): it resizes native buffers and calls `requestRender()`, but never emits a fresh `\x1b[2J` or sets `forceFullRepaintRequested`. The ONE `\x1b[2J` this app ever emits is at startup — `lastFrame()`'s "slice from the last clear" heuristic was therefore already just "slice from startup" for this whole file, tolerated by every `.toContain()` assertion (stale content matches fine) but broken for the one `.not.toContain()` assertion (resize compaction), since stale pre-resize columns never get erased from the raw byte log even though a real terminal would clip them at the new width.
- Fix: `PtySession` now records the byte offset of `output()` at the moment of each `resize()` call; `lastFrame()` takes the LATER of that offset and the last clear-screen index. This encodes the real guarantee (everything meaningful for the new geometry is written after resize() returns) without needing full terminal-grid emulation.
- Verified: the previously-failing `expect(compact).not.toContain("NAVIGATOR")` now passes. tsc clean; default suite 918/24/0 unaffected; 0 new lint findings.
