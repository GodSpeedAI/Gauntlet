## Known Limitations (recorded honestly)

1. **Bounds are workbench policy, not Prime capability.** `RecursionPolicy` (depth/children) is enforced by typed refusals BEFORE any drive because the installed adapter model exposes no native depth/budget control; it is never presented as a Prime capability (pinned by the capability-facts test). If the real Prime enforces its own limits, the two layers compose, and the workbench policy must not be relaxed on that assumption alone.
2. **The child's received context rides the mailbox as JSON.** In the in-process model the envelope+task is delivered as the child's first mailbox message; a real Prime integration may instead pass context through Prime's own context/attachment mechanism. The artifact (typed envelope) and its guarantees are unchanged; only the transport of the initial hand-off would differ.
3. **The delegation ledger is host-local.** `DelegationRecord`s live in the dispatcher instance; death after spawn with a lost ledger is recoverable from the adapter's real topology (`reconcileLedger`), but durable cross-process delegation bookkeeping belongs to the canonical write boundary (REQ-OPS-001/T12 territory) and is claimed nowhere here.
4. **One shared adapter for parallel investigations.** The Attack 1 suite uses two dispatchers over one adapter to model independent investigations; per REQ-PRIME-004 the constraint that matters (no concurrent execution against one single-threaded REPL) is enforced by T05's kernel serialization, which T10 never bypasses — no T10 path executes shell/Python at all.
5. **The deny-matrix re-run is deliberately small** (one allowed + two denied drives, per the task's scoping); it re-verified the delegation route against the T09 semantics without duplicating the full four-route matrix. The anchor-vs-unanchored re-drive refusal-shape difference logged in T09's round-2 trigger-records does not arise here because every recursion-suite drive is anchored.
6. **`PrimeSubstrateRecord` has no constructor.** The provenance-only record shape exists as a type contract for the task that adds a supported substrate; today every substrate request resolves `unsupported_substrate`, so no record can be (or is) produced.

### MAJOR 1 — `required_dependencies` was an uncapped smuggling channel; per-role context budgets were implemented nowhere (prime-context-envelope.ts)

- **Finding (executed probe: falsifier1.ts attack family A)**: the verifier declared every Case focus OUTSIDE the hoisted plan (their world: both unrelated units, all evidence, both sessions) as `required_dependencies` with plausible reasons. The envelope stayed `kind: "bounded"`, the delegation record recorded `envelope_kind: "bounded"`, and the child received whole-Case content — boundedness was a label, not a bound. Related: REQ-OPS-004's "Per-role context budgets apply after this mandatory closure" had no implementation at all.
- **Fix** (`src/prime-context-envelope.ts`): an explicit per-role context budget, applied AFTER the mandatory closure — `EnvelopeRoleBudget { relation, declared_dependency }` counting ONLY the two non-structural roles (horizon-relation items and declared dependencies); the mandatory closure roles and the single horizon root are exempt by definition (`BUDGET_EXEMPT_ROLES`).
