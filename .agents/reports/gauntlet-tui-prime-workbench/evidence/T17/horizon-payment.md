## Known gaps and confounds (recorded honestly)

1. **The fold itself is still O(E·U) internally.** `deriveRelations`
   (relations.ts, not owned this wave) scans all units per evidence row, so even
   a hoisted fold on LARGE costs ~117 ms round trip (vs ~182 ms whole-Case) and
   the off-path fold completion does not shrink proportionally to the horizon.
   Worker placement removes it from the render path; a linear-time unit index
   inside relations.ts would cut fold wall-time. Recorded as adjacent scope, not
   fixed here (scope discipline).
2. **WorkbenchModel.getSnapshot still folds inline.** The model class is
   T07/T08-owned and untouched this wave. T17 delivers the typed handoff
   contract, both executors, the coordinator, and measured composition proof
   against the real model/store; swapping the model's snapshot planner to consume
   the coordinator's accepted plan is the follow-up composition change in that
   file.
3. Focus switches under worker placement reuse the cached plan when the Horizon
   is unchanged (re-folding nothing); a switch that changes the Horizon is
   measured as a hoist interaction.
4. Peak RSS is sampled in-process at checkpoints (`process.memoryUsage().rss`);
   the worker process's RSS is not separately attributed.
5. Final target-scale budgets across every terminal/remote latency remain T21
   (prereg blindspot), as does REQ-GOAL-006's "current actions" dimension.
