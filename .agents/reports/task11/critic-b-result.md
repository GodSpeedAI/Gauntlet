Greps across `crates/` for `verify_claim` callers, `VerificationRefusal::Evidence`, `.deterministic()`, `unwrap_or*`, `record_evidence_production` callers, CLI wiring. No compilation, no tests run (mandate). "No evasion" is claimed only within this scope.

## Verdict

The load-bearing P12 core is real: absent evidence yields `Err(EvidenceMissing)` at every adapter, `INCONCLUSIVE + evidence_unresolvable` at the chain, and `NotPass` at the precondition; the proof test runs the real adapters, real sqlite store, real fs store. Write-once production records are enforced twice (pre-check + PK). No false PASS is reachable through the *request* path. However, I found two MAJOR evasions that hollow out the "earned chain" guarantee at other seams, one substantive content-binding gap, and several documented-but-unenforced vacuities.

## Findings

### MAJOR-1 — The §14.2 ladder is a per-call caller argument: a hand-built rung mints a genuine PASS `VerifiedClaim` with a forged instrument identity
- `crates/gauntlet-app/src/verify/authority.rs:261-266` — `pub fn verify_claim(&self, request: &VerificationRequest, ladder: &Ladder, ...)`. The rung set is supplied by the caller of *this* call, while the module doc (authority.rs:134-136) frames adapter substitution as composition-root-only.
- `crates/gauntlet-app/src/verify/ladder.rs:211` (`Ladder::new` is `pub`), `ladder.rs:137` (`LadderRung::new` is `pub`), `ladder.rs:244` — `select_and_verify` is only `pub(crate)`, but that gate is pointless when the whole ladder travels through the public `verify_claim`.
- Attack trace: implement `Verifier` (public trait) with `verify -> Ok(VerificationRecord::new(.., Outcome::Pass, InstrumentName::new("gauntlet-verify-test-report")))`, `instrument()` echoing the same name, `is_deterministic() -> true`, `supports() -> true` (default, verifier.rs:119-121). Pass one rung as the ladder to `verify_claim`. The chain's binding checks pass (they read durable state); the only instrument-side checks are `record.instrument() == rung.identity().name()` (authority.rs:351-357) and `record.evidence() == dispatched.evidence` (authority.rs:359-369) — **both compare two values the same caller supplied** (the rung's self-report vs the rung's echo of its dispatch). The chain then mints a `VerifiedClaim` with `Outcome::Pass`, instrument `gauntlet-verify-test-report`, `deterministic: true` — byte-identical in trust position to a real verification, though no instrument examined the bytes. The P12 instrument-layer guarantee ("absent artifact → EvidenceMissing → INCONCLUSIVE") is equally bypassed: a lying rung never reports absence. Note this regresses Task-10's own pattern: `AdmittedComparison` has *no* public constructor for exactly this reason (state_store.rs:386-390), and the ladder doc (ladder.rs:241-243) claims "a caller cannot drive a bare ladder over its own request" — but it can drive the whole chain over its own ladder.
- Also note: grep shows **no production caller** of `verify_claim`/`for_run` anywhere outside the p12 test — the "composition root injects the ladder" claim is currently aspirational; the signature is the only contract.
- Fixed would be proven by: the ladder (or the rung set) becoming a field of `VerificationAuthority`/`GovernedVerification` injected at construction (or a non-constructible `LadderCapability` à la `AdmittedComparison`), plus a rung-name registry so `InstrumentName::new` free-text cannot impersonate a real adapter.

### MAJOR-2 — The recency anchor is a caller-chosen iteration, validated against nothing durable; and the production's iteration is a self-declared fact the store never checks
- `authority.rs:162-167` — `for_run(run_id, iteration_no, ...)` takes the iteration as a parameter; `binding_class` (authority.rs:523) decides staleness as `production.iteration_no().get() < self.iteration_no.get()`. Nothing cross-checks the governed iteration against the run's durable loop state (`StateStore::resume`/latest checkpoint exists and is never consulted).
- Attack trace: evidence produced at iteration 1 is correctly stale when verifying at iteration 2 (p12 test 4). The same caller calls `for_run(..., iteration 1, ...)` and the identical production is now "fresh" → PASS. "Stale" is relative to an unvalidated coordinate, so claim 3's invariant ("stale evidence → evidence_stale") holds only for callers who anchor honestly. The doc (authority.rs:209-215) openly parks this as "the COORDINATE of the question" — a recorded position, but the invariant as claimed ("no stale evidence", spec §14.3) is not enforced against durable state.
- Second half: the *store-minted event offset* only orders candidates (authority.rs:290, sqlite `ORDER BY event_log_offset`); the iteration compared is the **caller-recorded** `iteration_no` from `EvidenceProduction` (ports state_store.rs:161). Since `record_evidence_production` is ungated (see MAJOR-3), a writer can record iteration N for iteration-1 bytes; the minted offset launders nothing — it is fresh, but it certifies only the write, not the claimed production time. "Produced BEFORE the transform, recorded AFTER" therefore passes whenever the declared iteration ≥ governed iteration; within-iteration ordering (spec: "at or after the transform it certifies") is unimplementable today — no transform event exists in `RunEventKind` (ports state_store.rs:67-98).
- Fixed would be proven by: `for_run` deriving or validating the iteration from the durable checkpoint; and the store (or Task-12 wiring) refusing a production whose declared iteration exceeds the run's durable current iteration.

### MAJOR-3 — `record_evidence_production` is an ungated public write: forged provenance for any store-resolvable bytes (self-declared residual, but it is the trust root of every §14.3 check)
- ports `state_store.rs:337-341` records the residual honestly ("composition-root-wired with no role check in the store"); sqlite `state_store.rs:1379-1454` implements write-once faithfully (pre-check before append; PK `(run_id, evidence_ref)` in migration 0006; identical re-record idempotent returning the ORIGINAL anchor; conflicting refused; crash window documented).
- Attack trace: any code holding the `Arc<dyn StateStore>` writes `EvidenceProduction::new(ref_of_any_existing_bytes, Identity("gauntlet-verify-test-report", ..), required_locator, current_iteration, now)`. Every §14.3 binding check then passes by construction — producer (class-only, see MINOR-2), locator (by construction), recency (self-declared iteration, see MAJOR-2), resolution (the bytes exist). Combined with MAJOR-1, a full false-PASS `VerifiedClaim` + `SettlementReadiness` requires only: store handle + a file at the locator + pinned bytes. Write-once per `(run, ref)` does not help: a *fresh ref for the same bytes* is trivially recordable (content-addressing forbids nothing), and the same ref under a different run is a new key.
- Grep confirms no production caller of `record_evidence_production` exists yet (only adapter self-tests and the p12 fixture) — so this is a seam Task 12 must close, exactly as the builder recorded. I flag it because claims 1 and 3 lean on "durable state" as if durability implied trustworthiness; it does not while the writer is ungated.
- Fixed would be proven by: capability-gating the write to the evidence-owner role at the store (as `bind_observation_bundle`'s doc already aspires to), or minting production facts from the observation pipeline rather than accepting them as arguments.

### MAJOR-4 — The instrument verifies filesystem bytes at the locator; the record and pin name the store ref: nothing binds the verified content to the pinned content
- Chain: binding layer resolves `production.evidence()` in the store (authority.rs:474-494); the dispatched instrument reads `production.locator()` on the **filesystem** (all four adapters, e.g. test-report lib.rs:85 `contained_path(&self.root, claim.evidence.as_str())`); the minted B.5 record carries `evidence = production.evidence()` (authority.rs:328, 610) and the precondition pins that ref (precondition.rs:147-171).
- Attack trace: put passing-report bytes at `p12-report.json` in the workspace; put *different* bytes (or arbitrary bytes) in the store and record the production naming the store ref. The chain resolves the store ref (any bytes), the instrument passes on the file, the record certifies the store ref, settlement pins the store ref. The bytes pinned as "the evidence that passed" were never examined, and the bytes examined were never pinned. Contrast `ObservationBinding` (ports state_store.rs:118-146), which the same codebase binds ref **plus content digest** for exactly this reason — no analogous digest exists for verification evidence.
- Fixed would be proven by: a digest of the instrument-read bytes recorded on the production binding and re-derived by the chain (mirroring `ObservationBinding`), or instruments that read through the evidence store.

### MAJOR-5 — `settlement_precondition` takes the evidence store as a per-call argument: condition 2 is re-derived from a caller-selected oracle
- precondition.rs:129-134 — `evidence: &dyn EvidenceStore` is a parameter. The doc says state is "never admitted on say-so", but a stooge store (`get -> Some(vec![0])`, `is_pinned -> Ok(true)`, both trivial impls of a public trait) satisfies conditions 1-2 for any PASS `VerifiedClaim`. Bounded by needing a PASS claim first (real chain or MAJOR-1), but it makes `SettlementReadiness` — advertised as having "no public constructor... a fact the layer earned" — assemble-able from a forged oracle. The chain itself holds `Arc<dyn EvidenceStore>` (authority.rs:137-141); the precondition should take the claim's store, not a fresh argument.
- Fixed would be proven by: `SettlementReadiness` minting only against the store the `VerifiedClaim`'s chain holds (e.g. carried inside the claim), or a non-constructible store capability.

### MINOR findings (hardening / vacuity / fidelity)

1. **Vacuous locator check (self-documented).** authority.rs:460-473 compares `production.locator() != requirement.locator()` immediately after the candidates were fetched **by that exact locator** (authority.rs:278-281; sqlite `WHERE locator = ?2`). The comparison can never fire in either backend; §14.3 condition 4 is enforced by query construction, not by a check. Corollary: p12 test 7 (`p12...rs:900-942`), titled "evidence at another locator", actually exercises the *no-production-at-locator* path — a true mismatch is unobservable by construction, so the test silently proves the weaker claim.
2. **Producer check is class-only and one-directional.** authority.rs:503-516: `Producer::Instrument` is satisfied by *any* `InstrumentIdentity` (the durable producer name is compared to nothing; there is no name on the requirement to compare — recorded decision, faithful to the two-value vocabulary of §5.3.2). It can only ever fire for `Producer::Human` requirements. Model-produced evidence (producer `gauntlet-verify-model`) would satisfy an `instrument` requirement at the binding layer; only §14.2 ordering keeps the model behind deterministic rungs.
3. **`RungKind::deterministic()` is dead code; the recorded flag is self-attestation.** ladder.rs:100-102 is never called in any production path (grep). The B.5 `deterministic` flag comes from `rung.verifier().is_deterministic()` (authority.rs:371-375, 413-417). Nothing checks kind-vs-verifier consistency, so `LadderRung::new(RungKind::FileDigest, <model verifier>)` puts a model at position 1 (it would still be flagged false — the flag itself is honest; the *ordering* claim "deterministic-first is STRUCTURAL" rests solely on the composition root labeling rung kinds correctly). Likewise `contract/verifier.rs:253` compares `verifier.is_deterministic()` to `factory.deterministic()` — two values the same adapter author supplied; the suite cannot detect a lying flag.
4. **Evidence-store read failures are classed as binding failures; the typed refusal is dead.** authority.rs:476-486 converts `evidence.get` errors into `(EvidenceUnresolvable, ...)`, i.e. a transient `ReadFailed` becomes a durable INCONCLUSIVE/evidence_unresolvable record. `VerificationRefusal::Evidence` (authority.rs:80-82) is never constructed anywhere (grep). Contrast the precondition's fail-closed `PinMembershipUnknown`. Not a false PASS, but "unanswerable" is silently degraded into an answerable class, inconsistently with the layer below it.
5. **Model-rung result checks omit role and iteration.** ladder.rs:443-476 checks invocation id, run id, envelope digest, payload schema; it never checks `result.role() == RoleId::Verifier` nor `result.iteration_no()`. `ModelRung` also hardcodes `IterationNo::new(1)` for every dispatch (ladder.rs:373) regardless of the governed iteration, so the durable role_result records a wrong iteration for every model verification above iteration 1. `adopt_model_decision` (ladder.rs:503-542) does catch the determinism lie, foreign run/unit, and requirement drift; the un-checked `document.evidence()` is back-stopped by authority.rs:359.
6. **`verifier_envelope` builds JSON by raw interpolation** (ladder.rs:553-570): a claim/locator containing `"` yields a malformed-JSON envelope body; `ContextEnvelope::new` (agent_runner.rs:235-237) does not validate. Digest still binds the exact bytes, so no identity forgery — just a corrupt-context hazard.
7. **`is_pinned` follows symlinks / TOCTOU.** evidence-fs store.rs:294-300: `object_path(&hex).exists()` follows symlinks (unlike `get`, which refuses them at 195-212). Bounded because the precondition calls `get` first and would fail closed; still, `is_pinned` alone would report `true` for an aliased object.
8. **Answer-key locator fidelity of the proof itself.** AK-005's frozen locator is `tests/proofs/p12_missing_is_not_passing` (repo-root) but the proof lives at `crates/gauntlet-cli/tests/p12_missing_is_not_passing.rs` — the header (p12...rs:9-22) records the boundary-suite conflict and substitutes a *test-target name* equivalence. Likewise AK-035/036's locators (`tests/contract/verifier::...`) correspond to no materialized path; their evidence lives in in-crate unit tests. Under the system's own §14.3 rule, these units' declared evidence does not resolve at its locator — the proofs prove the properties but would not verify their own units without the recorded mapping.
9. **B.5 shape deviations (defensible, undeclared):** annex shows `"produced_at": ""` and `"iteration_no": 0`; the codec accepts only a non-negative integer `produced_at` and iteration ≥ 1 (verification_record.rs:491-540). Stricter-than-annex, fine, but it is a silent narrowing of the documented wire shape.

## What I could not break (within scope)

- `VerificationRecordDoc::new` refuses class-on-settled-outcome at construction and decode (verification_record.rs:379-389, 838-855); unknown-key rejection holds at document, requirement, and instrument levels (tests at 751-776); payload form refuses a `schema` key and standalone decode refuses its absence.
- `VerifiedClaim` and `SettlementReadiness` have private fields and no public constructors; `GovernedVerification` is not `Clone`.
- Frozen-reference open is digest-verified against the durable run's pinned `ReferenceVersion` (comparator.rs:1039-1093); tampered-bytes test exists (authority.rs:703-718).
- Write-once production records: pre-check before append, PK, idempotent-same/conflicting-different, event replay anchored to the row's offset (sqlite state_store.rs:1379-1454, tests 2302-2432).
- fs evidence store: content re-hash on `get`, symlink/dir refusal, write-once collisions, fail-closed `is_pinned` default at the port.
- The p12 proof is genuinely composed (real adapters, real sqlite DB file, real fs store, pinned+digested frozen key); its assertions are outcome/class/refusals, no `is_ok()`-where-refusal-claimed patterns; deterministic-first is proven with a dispatch counter, not just an identity assertion.
- No `unwrap_or` collapses unanswerable into answerable in the chain (only the two benign sites listed); `Outcome` retains the no-boolean-conversion discipline (domain values.rs:298-302).

**Priority to fix:** MAJOR-1 and MAJOR-5 (close the ladder/store argument seams — cheap, and they are the only findings that let a caller *mint* trust rather than degrade it), then MAJOR-4 (digest-bind verified content to pinned content), then MAJOR-2/3 (Task-12 scope, already half-recorded by the builder).
